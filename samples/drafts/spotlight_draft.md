# Nonprofit Spotlight: Harbor Pathways

Last year, Harbor City Gives raised over $2.3 million for 270 local nonprofits, and this year we want to go even further!

Since 2023, we've placed more than 500 young adults in careers with family-sustaining wages.

Harbor Pathways graduates stay: 92% were still employed one year after placement.

This Giving Day, Harbor Pathways is raising $250,000 to give 10 young adults a complete career pathway, including training, mentorship and job placement.

They work with nearly 40 local employers who are ready to hire.

Just $2,500 covers transportation for an entire cohort.

That's 10 lives changed.

During Match Week, every gift is matched up to $100,000, so your donation goes twice as far!
